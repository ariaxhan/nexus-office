## 1. Verdict

- **Office (door + Mac app + phone + Ask) works.** Door 361/365 liveness samples, self-recovered
  every wedge; `/api/health` 200 at head `346731b` today; Ask 24/24 replies; gate-by-id, Host/origin
  checks, webhook HMAC, GitHub budget and the shot harness have held since August.
- **Tower as an autonomous issue worker does not pay for itself.** Whole life (09-03..10-02):
  24 effective merged PRs across 7 repos, ~3.6k lines, 16 of them in one burst 09-13..09-15.
  On nexus-office it is 12 of 302 commits (~4%), ~3% of added lines. Nothing landed since 09-27.
- **Tower coordination is failing right now**, and its own status table shows mostly green.
- **Not distributable as-is.** Blockers are safety (agents run unsandboxed on issue text), personal
  coupling (290 references to this machine's tools), no installer, and unbounded ledger growth.

`owner_exited` mechanism: a parent kills the lane. `dispatch_wave` gives children the same budget
as itself and SIGKILLs at its own timeout (`work.py:770`); plan timeouts kill the runner; the two
clocks disagree across Mac sleep. `remaining()` hands out whatever budget is left with no floor,
which is how a `gh issue comment` got a 0.066 s timeout.

## 5. Root causes

1. **Success is measured by proxies.** Exit 0, "produced", "dispatched", "settled". Almost every
   class-A incident is a proxy passing while the outcome failed. The repo's own rule ("a build
   passing proves nothing about a screen") was never applied to Tower.
2. **One shared checkout for humans and agents.** Leases, write-sets, held branches, owner
   reconciliation and `resolving` exist only to make this safe. They are most of `work.py`
   (1,467 lines, 39 edits since 09-11) and most of classes C and I.
3. **The system rebuilds itself while running.** Tower merges its own changes, a job redeploys every
   10 minutes, and the thing being fixed is the thing doing the fixing. 48 of 84 Tower commits since
   09-11 are fixes. The TBS coordinator went through 6 architectures in 18 days.
4. **Failure is either silent or a flood.** Quarantine tells nobody; probes comment 495 times.
   Nothing in between reaches the one person who can act.
5. **Scope outran the stable core.** 499 commits in 5.5 weeks. Podcasts, lessons, care, money-swarm,
   remote desktop and an autonomy roadmap (#196..#210) were added on top of a lane that was landing
   nothing.

## 6. What it got right (keep)

- Loopback-only door with Host, origin and JSON checks; HMAC webhook that refuses without a secret.
- Gate answered by question id; a gate is never hidden.
- GitHub as a budget; last-good data with "as of".
- Ledger design: WAL, append-only and terminal-state triggers, zero corruption since 09-04.
- "Nothing stays local-only": held work is pushed, failed flights keep their log.
- Independent reviewer flights: caught four real defects before merge on 09-26.
- `_card` contract, StateRules with 99 tests, the shot harness.
- Durable Ask queue and the single human-input path (survived a v3 migration).

## 7. Path to distribution-grade

Recommendation: **ship Office, not Tower.** The distributable is the face: door, Mac app, phone
page, Ask, Watch and Find over the user's own repos. Tower's coding lane stays a personal
experiment, off by default, until it meets the bar in step 2.

**Step 0. Stop the bleeding (about 3 agent-hours)**
- Write `work.issue` and `work.disposition` only when the content changes; add event retention.
- Quarantine raises one human ask; a plan that cannot start is a different error from a plan that
  failed.
- Coordinator direct claims refuse `waiting on human` and money-labelled issues; back off after 2
  releases with outcome `open`.
- Floor under `remaining()`; children get less budget than the parent that kills them.
- Decide the 3 quarantined plans, the 12 stale PRs and the 77 held branches (deletion needs Aria).

**Step 1. Freeze and cut (about 6 agent-hours)**
- Feature freeze on `nexus/`. Hold the autonomy roadmap (#196..#210) until step 2 passes.
- Stop the 10-minute self-deploy; deploy on a tag, one release directory, one Python.
- Split the tree: generic core (~19k Python lines) / personal integrations (~6.5k, optional
  plugins) / personal automation (~3.9k: podcasts, lessons, care, money-swarm, TBS; out of the
  product). Lists in the distribution lane's report, section 4.
- Remove hard dependencies on `~/Developer/Vaults`, `account-router.sh`, `jobctl`, `hcom`,
  `tbs-*` from core paths.

**Step 2. Prove stability with outcome numbers (7 calendar days of normal use)**
- Office gate: 7 days with zero manual repairs, door restart and Mac sleep/wake exercised, physical
  iPhone journeys done (#178, #179 step 1).
- Tower gate, if it is kept: landed-and-verified per executed flight over 0.5 (today 4 of 70), zero
  flights on human-only issues, zero silent stops, ledger growth under 10 MB a day.
- Status shows outcomes (merged commits, artifacts), never flight counts.

**Step 3. Make it installable (about 12 agent-hours plus Apple account steps)**
- Developer ID signing, hardened runtime, notarization; bundled pinned Python; `SMAppService`
  agents for the door (and Tower if kept); updater; uninstaller; first-run setup that discovers
  repos and checks `gh`, `claude`, `codex`.
- Per-install token for loopback writes. Remote desktop and Funnel opt-in.
- Clean-machine test: a second macOS user account with none of the Vaults tooling.

**Step 4. Only if Tower ships to anyone else**
- No `--dangerously-skip-permissions` or `danger-full-access` on issue text
  (`nexus/executor.py:131-158,260`, `client/office_ask.py:771`).
- One isolated clone or worktree per flight. This deletes the lease, write-set and held-branch
  machinery rather than hardening it, and needs a ruling against I0.14.
- No label-triggered merge to a default branch without a human.

## 8. Decisions for Aria

1. Ship Office without Tower (recommended), or hold distribution until Tower meets the step 2 bar.
2. Per-flight isolation for Tower (reverses the no-worktrees rule for this runner).
3. Delete the 77 held branches and close the 12 stale Tower PRs.
4. Turn off the TBS coordinator direct-claim loop until it refuses human-only issues.
